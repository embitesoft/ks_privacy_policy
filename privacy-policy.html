Kissan Saathi V2 — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Kissan Saathi V2 is a mobile application designed to communicate with Kissan Saathi IoT motor-controller devices and provide farmers with remote monitoring and control of their agricultural motors.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use the Kissan Saathi V2 application.

2. Information We Collect

To provide the application's functionality, the Kissan Saathi system may collect and process the following information:

Mobile Number

Your mobile number may be collected for user registration, authentication, device association and communication-related services.

Kissan Saathi Device Information

The system may collect information associated with the Kissan Saathi device, including:

  • Kissan Saathi Device ID
  • SIM/mobile number associated with the device
  • IMEI
  • 4G/network information
  • Motor/device status

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Kissan Saathi V2 does not require or collect:

  • Your name
  • GPS or precise location information
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Microphone recordings
  • Advertising profile information

4. How We Use Information

The collected information is used only as necessary to provide and operate Kissan Saathi V2 services, including:

  • Registering and associating users with Kissan Saathi devices
  • Establishing communication between the mobile application and Kissan Saathi hardware
  • Providing remote motor ON/OFF control
  • Displaying motor and device status
  • Providing timer-control functionality
  • Providing device and motor-related alerts/status information
  • Monitoring communication and network status
  • Supporting troubleshooting and system operation

5. MQTT Communication

Kissan Saathi V2 uses MQTT Hive as an MQTT communication service for exchanging device-related information between the application/server infrastructure and Kissan Saathi IoT devices.

Information transmitted through MQTT may include device identification, motor commands, motor/device status and network-related information required for the operation of the system.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted through the communication infrastructure and services required to operate Kissan Saathi V2.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental authority.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with Kissan Saathi V2 against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain Kissan Saathi V2 services, maintain device associations, support the operation of the system, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. User Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request information regarding their personal data and may request correction or deletion where applicable.

Requests can be made using the contact information provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Kissan Saathi V2 is an agricultural motor-control application and is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, services, technology or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated policy will be made available through the published Privacy Policy page.
